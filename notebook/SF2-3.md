### 2026-10-08T18:06-07:00 — surprise: one hash cannot say what changed

The plan asks for upstream-stale "by its basis hash", but a verdict stores one hash, and a
changed hash does not say which input changed. Telling "only a fact in another root changed"
apart from "this fact changed" needs a second stored value. Proposed: a verdict's `upstream`
map, `{<full reference>: <basis>}` for the facts in other roots its fact rests on, reached through
same-root references until they cross (so a fact resting on another root through a same-root
inference is covered too). Upstream-stale means: recomputing the basis with the recorded upstream
bases gives back the verdict's basis, and at least one upstream basis moved. A current verdict's
map must match exactly. Without the map, an upstream change reads as plain stale, which fails
safe. The design's Freshness section records it as pending decision; schema and code implement it.

### 2026-10-08T18:06-07:00 — surprise: `relates` can form cycles

Premises are acyclic (SF2-2's check), but `relates` is not: `same-as` both ways is natural. The
design's formula recurses through every referenced fact, so it is undefined on a cycle.
Proposed: hash a strongly connected component as one (a cycle digest over its members' local
parts and their outside inputs); outside cycles the formula is unchanged. A cycle across two
roots makes the upstream classification fail safe (stale), tested with a crafted record.
Recorded as pending decision.

### 2026-10-08T18:06-07:00 — decision: what the implementer settled within the design

Listed for the orchestrator to confirm:

- **Unknown, a fifth status.** A basis that cannot be established (a dangling or failed
  reference, a cited name the file does not list, a cited path missing from `files`, or resting
  on such a fact) is unknown: never current; a warning, an error under either mode.
- **Which findings untrust a root.** A record's own defects and a current FAIL are data defects
  and count; they run before the trust pass. Freshness findings (stale, unverified, unknown, a
  missing second reader) are a policy on the checked root: reported for checked roots only,
  after the trust pass. So a docs root with unverified facts never blocks the GPL root's
  references into it.
- **A missing second reader** is a warning, an error under both modes (the worked example's
  text: "under `--require-verified` the check also asks for a second reader"); reported only on a
  current verdict, so a stale one reports stale alone.
- **ADJUDICATE** current is a warning in every mode, never an error (format 1's rule).
- **GAP** is the verdict of a gap fact, both ways (facts only; instance and variant rows have no
  gap notion).
- **Readers who disagree** with a non-ADJUDICATE verdict are an error: the record would read as
  confirmed while a reader dissents.
- **Carried verdicts:** from a format 1 record, `contrary_evidence` and `citation_precision` are
  absent (the design's rule); from a format 2 record, required like any format 2 verdict.
- **Sub-keys** (`id.sub`) are refused until SF2-7 gives a kind fields or steps with their own
  support.
- **Records outside `resources/`**, or named like a record but not `*.verify.yaml` (a format 1
  `.verify.md`, `.verify.yml`, a case variant, a backup), are errors, like misnamed specs.
- **`status`** takes `check`'s root options (`--require-license` and `--public-skill` decide which
  roots are trusted, so they change statuses) and exits 1 when the check found an error.
