# L02f3: spec feedback, reverification and L02 acceptance

## Terms

- **Candidate** — `e1000_l02`, the driver written from spec revision 4 alone (L02e) and
  repaired once in L02f2. **Reference** — Linux v6.12's `e1000`.
- **Spec revision 6** — revision 5 ([L02s](L02s.md)) plus this unit's changes; the spec is
  a clean-room artifact, edited from the manual, the spec and permitted observations only.
- **`[emulated]`** — the design's proposed evidence class for a result observed on the QEMU
  device model, weaker than a hardware measurement ([design](../QEMU-DIFFERENTIAL.md),
  "Evidence class"). Revision 6 adds it to the spec's own tag table.
- **Acceptance stage** — the design's A1–A7, checked against the whole of L02's evidence
  here; formerly the separate unit L02g.
- **Isolated run**, **claim**, **qualified** — as in the [L02d3 evidence](L02d3.md#terms);
  **divergence** and its labels as in the [L02f1 evidence](L02f1.md#terms). **Verifier** and
  **verification record** as in `spec-verifier` and the [L02s evidence](L02s.md#terms).

See the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md), the [design](../QEMU-DIFFERENTIAL.md), the
[plan](../IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) (L02f3) and the [notebook chapter](../notebook/L02f3.md).

## The `[emulated]` class: decision from use

Recommendation: add `[emulated]` to `SPEC-FORMAT.md` and the design's evidence model, as a
separate named follow-on (SF-1 below), not in this unit. Revision 6 used the class in three
shapes that held up: a pointer from a register row or a step to a numbered observation with
its run IDs (§4.1, R6, E1 → §12.5); one premise inside an `[inference]` argument, never the
sole authority (E1, R6); and a table of observations each carrying the consequence for a
driver (§12.5). Two rules emerged: the class needs the model's version and the run IDs the
way `[hardware]` needs the board, and an observation must be phrased as what was observed
from outside the model, never as its mechanism, or the tag becomes a channel for the model's
source. The change touches the format's consumers and checks: `spec_check.py` enumerates the
tag names it accepts (`TAG_NAMES`) and the format says which tags need a verification clause,
so adding the class is a checker change with tests, which the plan says makes it a separate
unit.

## Limitations

- All four spec verifiers, the acceptance reviewer and the implementer share a model family; no
  human read the spec or the runs in this unit.
- The manual was read in its text rendering; no quoted value here depends on a symbol that
  rendering loses (the micro sign is spelled out where it matters).
- The acceptance runs are two repetitions per scenario on one host with KVM under the same
  concurrency as L02f2b; nothing here measures behavior under other load or with TCG.
- The candidate is a revision-4 driver with one repair; revision 6 has not been implemented.
- Subagent transcripts are recorded by path in the run's ledger; those paths are temporary.
