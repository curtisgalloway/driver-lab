## 2026-09-25T16:45-07:00 — decision: read isolation by bubblewrap, audit by strace

matters as much as the sandbox: the operator's Codex home holds old sessions and memories,

## 2026-09-25T17:14-07:00 — attempt: attributing down-during-traffic
Reference 6/6 PASS, and isolated runs rule out L02d2's cross-scenario leftovers (0 at every
bring-up). But the trace shows the missing reply *did* reach the candidate: accepted by the
model's filter, descriptor consumed and refilled, identically in passing and failing runs.
The first ping of every candidate run takes about 490 ms, the reference's under 1 ms. QEMU
10.2.1's `set_rx_control` arms a one-second timer on every RCTL write, and reception waits for
it. Both drivers rewrite RCTL at carrier-up (multicast join); the candidate reports carrier
0.5 s after open (LSC interrupt), the reference after 2 s (watchdog), so only the candidate's
"afterwards" ping lands inside the hold, together with 30-odd flood frames the peer sent after
the DUT went down.

## 2026-09-25T17:17-07:00 — result: the reply reaches ICMP
Six more candidate runs with a private harness copy that dumps `/proc/net/snmp` around the
pings: 5 FAIL, 1 PASS, and `Icmp: InEchoReps` rose by 3 in all six. The kernel received every
reply; ping lost one above ICMP while the burst was queued. Not a candidate defect: labeled V6,
`benign`, model limitation plus a harness weakness (H1: the check pings inside the hold after
a flood that leaves frames behind). No round 2; H1 left open for the user to place.

## 2026-09-25T17:45-07:00 — result: reviews, and two corrections to this chapter
`review-swarm` rerun with its working files in the run store: 13 findings, the notable ones a

differs is that its leftover flood frames were released two seconds earlier. Also at 17:14,
the seq-0 reply was not held: its request waited for the held ARP reply, and the reply came
back about 0.1 ms after the burst.
