# L02f2b — harness fix H1 and Q15 requalification

Unit of the [plan](../IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) (L02f). Terms: the **receive hold** is QEMU's
one-second stop on reception after any RCTL write; **H1** is L02f2's finding that
`down-during-traffic` pinged inside it. See the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) and the
[evidence](../evidence/L02f2b.md).

## 2026-09-25T18:05-07:00 — opening
Goal: fix H1 the way the revised plan says (traffic through shutdown, a bounded settling
interval after carrier, no ping retry) and requalify Q15 by L02d3's method. Start: branch
`driver-porting/l02f2b` from `origin/main` at `3dac0a3`, own worktree, clean. Private run
`e1000-l02f2b-20260925-01`.

## 2026-09-25T18:10-07:00 — the interval, from L02f2's traces
Nothing to change for "traffic through shutdown": the scenario already stops both floods
after the interface is down. In all 18 L02f2 traces the carrier-up RCTL write came before
the harness read carrier 1 (0.50 s for the candidate, about 1 s for the reference, whose
watchdog reports carrier just after the previous one-second poll), so the hold never
outlasted that read by more than 0.05 s. Chose 3 s, plus a trace-checked precondition so a
run that still pings inside a hold says so instead of failing a ping.

## 2026-09-25T18:16-07:00 — the old harness still fails at this load
Load control first, as declared: the old harness on the candidate, eight at a time, failed
4 of 8, the same check each time. The fixed harness then passed reference and candidate
10 of 10 each, and d15 failed 4 of 4, in 71 s for all 24 runs.

## 2026-09-25T18:19-07:00 — surprise: the reference's burst is earlier than its last RCTL write
The new check's detail counts leftover frames after the last RCTL write. For the reference
it reads 1 frame: its leftovers (47 to 57) come at 1.0 s, when the hold from open ends,
before carrier and before its carrier-up RCTL write at about 2 s. The check's verdict is
unaffected; the evidence counts from interface-up with a separate script.

## 2026-09-25T18:35-07:00 — correction after review: the reason for 3 s
The 18:10 entry says the hold "never outlasted that read by more than 0.05 s". That holds
for the reference in L02f2 only: a candidate write 0.50 s before the read ends its hold
about 0.5 s after it, and three of this unit's reference runs read carrier about 10 ms after
the write, so their hold ended about 1 s after the read. The interval stands (at least 2 s
between hold end and pings in every run); the reason is corrected in the evidence and the
harness comment. The reviewer also noted the new check cannot see an RCTL write once the
pings start; recorded as a limitation.
