## 2026-09-25T17:50-07:00 — the manual's case, and a tension
Table 13-31: bit 11 "Assert CRS on Transmit", reset 0b = "never assert on transmit", no effect
in full duplex, retained across a PHY software reset. §13.7.12: "The PHY should assert the
internal carrier sense signal during every transmission." §8.4.2 (half duplex, applies to the
82540EM): carrier sense is expected within one slot time, or the PHY may be "configured
incorrectly". The manual never says "set bit 11"; the spec now tags the conclusion
`[inference]` over three `[databook]` premises. The tension: §13.7.12 also says the TNCRS
counter is valid only in full duplex, where the bit has no effect, so TNCRS cannot confirm the
half-duplex case. The step's verification method says so. Revision 4 had called the bit
optional ("only if half-duplex links misbehave") in a `[source-observed]` note; that guidance
now points at G4.

## 2026-09-25T17:58-07:00 — result: review 1 found my ordering claim wrong
A fresh verifier (`spec-verifier`, changed claims plus every bit-11 claim): 11 PASS, 2 FAIL,
2 GAP; wall check on the changed hunks PASS; my leak-scan comparison confirmed. FAIL 1 is
mine: I argued bit 11 needs no order relative to the AN restart because its software-reset
column says "Retain". §11.1.3 names PSCON bit 11 explicitly among the bits that take effect
only at a PHY soft reset, AN restart, power-down exit or link loss; "Retain" says what a reset
keeps, not when a write applies. Checked in the manual text: the verifier is right. Lesson:
read the section that governs when a register write applies, not only the bit table.
FAIL 2: the argument leaned on §13.7.12, which describes a counter valid only in full duplex,
where the bit does nothing; it also assumed, silently, that bit 11 drives the carrier sense
the MAC watches; and it ignored §8.4.2's "completes successfully even if" carrier sense is
absent. Confidence drops to medium. The two GAPs: no observable in the verification method,
and the manual contradicts itself on TNCRS in half duplex (§8.4.2 says the event is counted
there; §13.7.12 says TNCRS is valid only in full duplex).
