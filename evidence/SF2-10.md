# SF2-10: bcm2711 converted and verified on draft branches

**Terms:**

- *carry*: copying a format 1 verdict onto the matching format 2 fact, recorded as `carried_from`,
  with no new reading.
- *fresh*: a fact that needs a new reading by a verifier.
- *critical*: a fact flagged as needing a second reader (D14).
- *citation pass*: a no-sources agent step that turns the converter's prose citations into
  structured support.
- *delta verification*: reading only the facts whose records are not current.

See the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the rest.

**Status: complete.** The three bcm2711 specs and their records are in format 2 on branch

hardware-specs-permissive `ad4ceaf`, hardware-specs-gpl `0181f34`. The branches are **not merged**;

citation pass, splits, fidelity, carry) and SF2-10b (delta verification).

## What was done

- **Survey and conversion.** The corpus survey (counts in the [notebook](../notebook/SF2-10.md))
  preceded conversion. Codex wrote `spec.py migrate`, which converts mechanically; all 65 v1
  verdict keys map to a v2 fact. An executing review of `migrate.py` found no blockers: its outputs
  were verified trustworthy and deterministic. Four parser gaps (S1-S4) do not occur in the bcm2711
  inputs and are recorded as limitations, because the script runs once and is deleted in SF2-12.
- **Citation pass, opening no source.** Three mixed bullets split into seven facts (D3). Critical
  marks, which the conversion dropped, were added: docs 11, permissive 3, GPL 3 facts.
- **Fidelity check, opening no source.** 50 of the 65 v1 verdicts carried (the plan's ceiling was
  61). Nine did not carry because v1 cited whole pages and the citation pass left a TODO; three
  were split bullets; three were uncertain and were kept fresh rather than carried. Carried
  records use `sources: []` since nothing was read for them. The three uncertain facts all later
  failed their first reading because the headings they cite do not exist.
- **Delta verification.** Sonnet primary readers on every fresh fact; Opus second readers on every
  critical fact, carried ones included. All carried critical facts passed. Three rounds of
  corrections followed. Under the user's overnight instruction, the three split verdicts were
  ruled FAIL.
- **Final state.** docs 35 PASS, permissive 15 PASS, GPL 19 PASS. `check --require-verified pr`
  and `main`: 0 errors and 0 warnings on all three roots together. `resolve` resolves 32 anchors
  (permissive) and 37 (GPL); `render` to Markdown and HTML pass.

## Consequential findings

- **Contrary evidence found by two independent readers.** The pinned `rpi-tools` `armstubs/Makefile`
  builds the high-peripheral stub (`-DHIGH_PERI`), while the documentation says stub support for
  that mode is missing. Resolution: the spec now attributes the statement to the documentation
  snapshot and records the Makefile in a hardware TODO; whether current firmware selects that stub
  is unknown. The docs root cannot cite the tools repository (documents-only license policy), so
  the Makefile lives in TODO text there.
- **Split verdicts failed.** Of the three, one met contrary evidence (the stub finding above), one
  rested on an undeclared default-mode premise, and one made a definite claim on a "likely"
  premise. Resolution: the premise was added, the claim hedged, the derivation made conditional.
- **Fresh readings found nonexistent headings.** The three uncertain carries cited headings
  typed from descriptive v1 locators ("opening sections", "CPU enable methods"). Resolution: real
  headings substituted. Keeping them fresh instead of carrying was the right call.
- **Conversion slips.** The datasheet note read "166-page PDF, ;" and the source-notices lead-in
  was dropped; both restored from v1.
- **Dropped critical marks** were re-added by the citation pass (counts above).
- **Repeat FAIL** on `no-el3-runtime-with-the-stock-stub` (a different defect each time) needed a
  third round: the author declared the two assumptions and a fresh reader passed it.
