Section 1: Reset
Reset completes in 10 clock cycles. This is a fixed count in this synthetic model.

Section 2: Post-reset settling
Immediately after reset completes, settling takes 4 more clock cycles before the
Widget is ready. This phase uses the same clock as reset and does not overlap reset.
