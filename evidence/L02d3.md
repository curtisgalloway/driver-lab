# L02d3: check qualification

## Terms

- **Harness** — [`evals/e1000/harness/`](../evals/e1000/harness/), unchanged from L02d2
  unless stated below. **DUT**, **peer**, **register trace**, **deferred check** and
  **planted defect** are as in the [L02d2 evidence](L02d2.md#terms).
- **Claim** — a check whose PASS L02f will cite as evidence about the candidate driver.
- **Precondition** — a check that only confirms a scenario's stimulus happened (a flood ran,
  a ring wrapped, the harness's own register write succeeded). Preconditions make a claim's
  result meaningful; they are not evidence about the driver and are not qualified here.
- **Qualified** — a planted defect that violates the claim made its check fail, for that
  reason, while the unmodified reference passed the same scenario on the same harness.
- **Labels for the rest:** `unqualified` (no defect was planted, or none made the check
  fail), `unobservable` (QEMU's model cannot show the violation), `insensitive` (a defect
  that violates the claim was planted and the check still passed; a finding about the check).
- **Isolated run** — a harness run of one scenario on freshly booted guests, so frames left
  over from an earlier scenario cannot reach it.

See the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md), the [design](../QEMU-DIFFERENTIAL.md) (§7, A6), the
[plan](../IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) (L02d3), and the [notebook chapter](../notebook/L02d3.md).

**Preconditions, not claims:** no frames left over during bring-up; memory BAR found; the

transmitting when the link dropped; traffic flowed both ways when the interface went down;

started; and the `capture` pseudo-scenario's checks.

**Summary.** Of 26 claims (24 frozen, 2 added after review and before any candidate run),
22 are qualified, most with the narrower scope stated in their row, and 4 are unqualified
(Q18, Q24, Q25, Q26). L02f may report unqualified claims but not cite them as qualified
evidence. d09 is an equivalent mutation (setting up the ring on open zeroes the indices
again) and a valid control; d16's corruption apparently never ran, so its equivalence is
inferred.

**Reused suite runs.** m2, m3 and m4 come from L02d2 suite runs, not isolated ones. Their
evidence here is confined to the first scenario, `01 smoke`, which runs on freshly booted
guests, so no earlier scenario can have contaminated it: m2 and m3 fail its pings; m4 writes
RDLEN=0xff0 (host time 1790368303.444527) and RDT=0xff (1790368306.451478) during it.

## Decisions

- **Qualified evidence comes from isolated runs.** L02d2's contaminated failures came from
  running the suite in one boot. New qualification runs used one scenario per boot; reused evidence is confined to the first
  scenario on fresh guests, as documented under the table. L02f
  cites a claim as qualified only from an isolated run of its scenario; a full-suite run
  stays valid for A3 (one command, unattended) but its results after a stalled flood are
  not qualified evidence. The harness already supports this (`--scenario` with a fresh
  `--out` per run); no drain step was added.
- **Claims grouped by behavior, not by check name.** One claim covers the same check in
  several scenarios (the pings after bring-up, for example); a defect in one scenario
  qualifies the check's logic, and the notes say where it was shown.
- **Q18 stays a check, unqualified.** Removing the wait did not produce a gap under 1 µs
  through the I/O window, so no defect here violated it; a candidate that resets through the
  memory BAR might. A check left unqualified is a finding, not a unit failure (plan). Its
  observation (the gaps) does distinguish the defect.
- **Claims added after the freeze.** Q25 and Q26 came from the review (the design lists
  60/61-byte reception and probe); both were added before any candidate run, which is what
  the freeze protects.

## Open limitations

- Qualification is defect-specific: each row shows one defect caught under these
  conditions, not that the check catches every violation of its claim.
- Unqualified claims (Q18, Q24, Q25, Q26) may be reported in L02f but not cited as qualified.
  (Q24, Q25 and Q26 were qualified in [QF-1](QF-1.md), 2026-09-26; Q18 stays unqualified there.)
- Q09 sees receive corruption only as a stalled transfer; corruption that TCP accepts, or on
  the transmit side, has not been shown detectable.
- Q07 and Q08 were qualified by defects that stop traffic before the ring wraps, not by a
  fault that appears only after a wrap. Q21 (tails) is qualified on the receive side only,
  Q20 through TDBAL, Q22 through RDH, Q05 at 1514 bytes.
- The full-suite run's contamination is avoided, not fixed: L02f must use isolated runs.
- The remaining L02d2 limitations (kernel-log pattern list, QEMU's overrun model, TCG
  untested, capture timestamps, same kernel on the peer) are unchanged.
