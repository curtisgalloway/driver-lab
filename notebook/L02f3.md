## 2026-09-25T18:43-07:00 — what an `[emulated]` entry may say
Writing §12.5 forced a rule: an entry states what the runs recorded (a register value, a
gap in a trace, frames in a capture), never the model's mechanism. The evidence files name
QEMU functions for the receive hold and the grant bit; the spec must not, or the tag becomes
a way for the model's source to cross the clean-room wall. Every entry got its run IDs the
way a `[hardware]` fact gets its board, and E1 and R6 use one as a premise, never alone.

## 2026-09-25T19:04-07:00 — the fix pass made three new defects
The second verifier failed four claims, three of them mine from the fix pass: the rename of
M1–M7 to EM1–EM7 was done by matching table rows starting with `| M<n> |`, which also
matched §11's milestone table, so the milestones were renamed and every "verify at M<n>"
reference dangled; a second replacement of a phrase already replaced produced "EEM7"; and
the header did not name the attestation edit. Lesson: a mechanical rename in a spec needs
its hits reviewed one by one (the user-level rule about mechanical renames applies to prose
tables too), and the header's change list must be rewritten from the final diff, not from
memory of the edits. The fourth: EM3 said "the only RCTL writes observed", which the
observations file does not support. A third fresh verifier follows.
