# SF2-9: peripheral, review and investigator skills in format 2

**Terms:**

- *record*: a structured entry that carries a fact's evidence; *payload*: the register, sequence,
  layout or review values a record holds.
- *root*: the marker file that declares a spec repository's license policy.
- *sub-key*: the key of an independently supported field or step, verified on its own.
- *trace review*: a reviewer takes each rule a document states and finds the schema line, code
  line or design line that makes it true; a rule with none is *untraceable*.
- *enforced / design-only*: a traced rule is enforced when a schema or checker rejects its
  violation, design-only when only the design document backs it.
- *format 1 text*: the earlier Markdown procedure, kept in a `FORMAT-1.md` in each skill until
  SF2-12.

See the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the rest.

## What was built

- **`peripheral-spec`:** describes and produces format 2 peripheral specs: registers, sequences and
  layouts as record payloads, `role` instead of pin lines, the `spec.py` commands (`validate`,
  `check`, `resolve`, `show`, `drift`, `inventory`), and the facts-file mode. The placement rule and
  license guidance are kept. The anchor-grammar section is replaced by the records contract in
  `spec-format`.
- **`reference-driver-review`:** reviews as format 2 records with `requirement` and `assessment`,
  correspondence pairs and coverage, and companion peripheral or facts files for register
  inventories. Its subagent and verifier prompt templates are rewritten around records.
- **`hardware-investigator`:** the facts-file procedure and template in format 2, and a worked
  example whose two expected answers (one per destination license) validate, check, resolve and
  assemble into specs.
- **Format 1 text:** each skill's original text, and its original prompt templates, moved unchanged
  to a `FORMAT-1.md` under a labeled preface. The `spec-verifier` and `board-expert` format 1 files
  were repointed.
- **Other:** four `GLOSSARY.md` rows; `test_sf2_9_docs.py`, which runs the documented commands.

## Reviews

| Round | Reviewer | Outcome |

Consequential findings and their resolution:

- **Worked example could not assemble (Codex).** The expected answers carried the init steps
  without sequence payloads, preserving a conversion omission from SF2-7a. Resolution: sequence
  payloads added, and a test assembles both answers into specs that validate, check and resolve.
- **Comment attribution against word-for-word fidelity.** SF2-7a's expected answers kept the
  upstream wording; the contract says a statement taken from a code comment is attributed to the
  comment and its anchor carries `comment: true`. Orchestrator decision under the user's overnight
  instruction: the contract wins. The LCR statement now names the source comment, content unchanged.
- **Fixture roots contradicted the placement rule (Opus).** Both fixture root markers declared an
  Apache-2.0 license while accepting GPL source, and the facts files' SPDX headers did not match
  their destinations. Nothing checks that a root's own license fits its accepts list. Orchestrator
  decision: fix the fixture roots and headers rather than add a checker rule in a docs unit. The
  Linux answer belongs to the GPL root, the firmware answer to the permissive one, and each header
  matches its destination.
- **Rules the text overstated (Opus).** `extra` findings were said to need a reference search
  anchor, but the checker requires only some reference-side anchor and the design does not say
  more; a search scope (file or `dir/`) must also be listed in the entry's `files`; canonical hex is
  lowercase; `resolve` does not skip an unbound repository, it tries to fetch it; peripheral-spec did
  not document `validate`, the root's `layer` and `name`, or the hex-claim warning. All corrected
  to match the checker, and four glossary rows added.
- **Pointer to a retired format 1 worked example.** The investigator's `FORMAT-1.md` pointed at a
  worked example that is now format 2. Its banner now says no format 1 example remains.

## Decisions

**Orchestrator, under the user's overnight instruction:** comment attribution follows the contract
over SF2-7a's word-for-word fidelity; the fixture roots' licenses were fixed rather than adding a
checker rule. No stop rule fired.

## Limitations and follow-ups

- **README, DESIGN and GLOSSARY still describe these skills in format 1 terms** (README's skill
  table, DESIGN's skill descriptions, two glossary rows). They are outside SF2-9's in-scope list,
  as they were for SF2-8. Tracked in SF2-12's section of the plan.
- **Rules not enforced by tooling.** Of 135 traced rules, 43 are design-only: backed by the design
  or the contract but not rejected by any checker. Two were untraceable or contradicted and are
  fixed above.
- **A padded anchor passes.** A register cited with extra neighboring lines passes `check`,
  `resolve` and `inventory`; the skills assign that judgment to the verifier.
- **Fixtures are synthetic.** The command tests and the worked example exercise tooling against
  synthetic sources; they are not driver or hardware results.
- **Out of scope here:** removing the format 1 scripts and tests (SF2-12), the board-spec skills
  (SF2-8), cutting over the spec repositories (SF2-11).
